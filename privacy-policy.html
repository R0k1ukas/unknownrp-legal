UNKNOWNRP.LT – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how UNKNOWNRP.LT Bot may collect, use and store information when you interact with it on Discord.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Username and server nickname.
  • Invitation and membership information.
  • Messages or information submitted through support tickets and forms.
  • Moderation records, such as warnings or bans.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide verification, moderation, invitation tracking, support tickets, announcements and other server management features.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information is stored only when necessary for the Bot's functionality. Retention periods depend on the feature and the server's configuration. Data should be deleted when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized UNKNOWNRP.LT administrators when necessary to manage the community and operate the Bot.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Requests

If you have questions or requests concerning information processed by the Bot, contact the UNKNOWNRP.LT server administration through its official Discord server.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
